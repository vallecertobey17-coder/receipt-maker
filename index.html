2Upload the student list

Word documents (.docx): attach the treasurer's list, one or several at once (e.g. one per section). Each document needs a COURSE, YEAR, & SECTION: box and a table with a NAME column; other columns like SIGNATURE and blank rows are ignored. From Google Docs: File → Download → Microsoft Word (.docx).
Spreadsheets (.xlsx) also work: make the column titles bold (e.g. Name, Course, Year & Section).

3Place the fields on the template

Each line of the receipt is already filled in: OR No. is generated, DATE is today, RECEIVED FROM and COURSE, YEAR & SECTION come from the list, the amount is ₱35 (in figures and in words), PAYMENT FOR is "Organizational Fee", and CASH is ticked. To change a fixed value (e.g. the amount), edit its text box below. Drag a box to move it, drag its right edge to change the width. Text that is too long shrinks to fit.
